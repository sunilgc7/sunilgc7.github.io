Biography

Researcher, educator, and engineer focused on AI-driven precision agriculture and computer vision.

Sunil G. C. is a researcher, educator, and expert in precision agriculture, artificial intelligence, and computer vision. He applies innovative technologies to agriculture, advancing automated systems, smart farming, and AI-based research. His interdisciplinary background spans agricultural engineering, robotics, and data science.

In industry, Sunil worked as a software engineer at Deerwalk Inc. in Kathmandu, contributing to big data systems with Cascading, Hadoop, and Elasticsearch, and optimizing cloud resource utilization. He also interned at AGCO – Appareo Systems in Fargo, ND, focusing on telemetry control systems and protocols like CAN bus and J1939.

As a researcher, he is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at USDA-ARS & North Dakota State University, leading projects on high-throughput phenotyping, hyperspectral data collection, and ML models for crop damage classification. He develops ROI labeling tools and trains algorithms for frost damage identification across crops.

Sunil’s academic work includes teaching and mentoring at North Dakota State University and Janakpur Engineering College, with guest lectures and hands-on instruction in precision agriculture, instrumentation, and embedded systems.
