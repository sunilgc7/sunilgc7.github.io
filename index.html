Biography

Assistant Professor of Sensing and Data Analytics · Department of Entomology and Plant Pathology.

Sunil G. C. is an Assistant Professor of Sensing and Data Analytics in the Department of Entomology and Plant Pathology at the University of Tennessee Institute of Agriculture. He joined in November 2026 and leads the GC PhytoEnto Sensing & AI Lab. He applies innovative technologies to agriculture, advancing automated systems, smart farming, and AI-based research. His interdisciplinary background spans agricultural engineering, robotics, and data science.

In industry, Sunil worked as a software engineer at Deerwalk Inc. in Kathmandu, contributing to big data systems with Cascading, Hadoop, and Elasticsearch, and optimizing cloud resource utilization. He also interned at AGCO – Appareo Systems in Fargo, ND, focusing on telemetry control systems and protocols like CAN bus and J1939.

Previously, he was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at USDA-ARS & North Dakota State University, working on automated phenotyping, hyperspectral imaging, and AI models for plant stress and frost damage. His current research connects sensing and early detection with targeted treatment, bringing tools from the lab to greenhouses, fields, and ground robots.

View UTIA faculty profile

Sunil’s academic work includes teaching and mentoring at North Dakota State University and Janakpur Engineering College, with guest lectures and hands-on instruction in precision agriculture, instrumentation, and embedded systems.
