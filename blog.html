Short reflections on AI in agriculture, imaging, and research practice.

From Lab Bench to Field Edge: Closing the Deployment Gap

January 2026

In the lab, models look clean. In the field, dust, wind, changing light, and mixed crops turn into noisy data. My current focus is building evaluation loops that include real deployment constraints early — edge inference latency, thermal limits, and shifting phenotypes across seasons. The goal is practical ML that survives a growing season, not just a leaderboard.

Why Hyperspectral Needs Better Labels

December 2025

We can collect extraordinary hyperspectral data, but without careful ROI labeling and consistent protocols, model performance collapses. I’m investing in semi-automated labeling tools and QA checks that treat labeling as a scientific experiment, not a data entry step.

Designing Robots That Farmers Actually Want

November 2025

It’s easy to design an impressive demo; it’s harder to design a tool that earns trust in the field. Reliability, repairability, and clear value per acre matter more than flashy autonomy. The best robotics projects I’ve worked on kept farmers in the loop from day one.
